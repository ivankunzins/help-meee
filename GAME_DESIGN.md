# SECRET VILLAGE — Game Design v0.1

## Core loop
1. Enter village.
2. Get 30 minutes to explore.
3. Find food / money / NPC clues / secret locations.
4. Spend money on clues, jobs and future equipment.
5. Find a secret and collect a permanent discovery.
6. Return next round with saved progress and search for something new.

## Retention
- 100-secret collection.
- Persistent money and items.
- Random wandering trader.
- Random events: rain, fog, night, blackout, unusual NPCs.
- New secrets requiring different tools.
- Multiplayer two-person puzzles.
- Daily/weekly challenges.
- Cosmetic collection and vehicle collection.

## Economy
Base earning is intentionally simple. The first job is janitor: $100 entry, $1 per leaf. Future jobs should have distinct gameplay rather than only higher money values.

Suggested jobs:
- Janitor: collect leaves.
- Fisher: catch fish at water.
- Courier: deliver packages before a deadline.
- Taxi driver: carry NPCs.
- Mechanic: repair cars.

## Secret progression
SECRET #001 is the underwater door. Later secrets should require combinations of clues, tools, events and locations. Avoid making every secret a random hidden button.

## Item progression
Common: flashlight, map, compass.
Rare: metal detector, diving gear, boat.
Epic: magic carpet, jetpack, advanced tools.
Legendary: special vehicles and unique exploration tools.
Mythic: world-changing secrets / cosmetics.

## Monetization principles
Robux should accelerate or customize rather than make the main secret impossible for free players. Examples: clue purchase, cosmetics, emotes, private-server extras, convenience boosts.

## Production roadmap
### Phase 1 — playable core
Village, timer, economy, seller, janitor, underwater secret, save system.
### Phase 2 — polish
Proper environment models, lighting, sound, animations, mobile UI, vehicle handling.
### Phase 3 — content
10+ secrets, random events, more NPCs, jobs, tools, inventory.
### Phase 4 — social
Co-op puzzles, trading, badges, leaderboards, parties.
### Phase 5 — live game
100 secrets, rotating events, weekly challenges, seasonal content, cosmetics and analytics.
